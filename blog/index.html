2BHK Interior Design

Planning a Modern 2BHK Interior

A 2BHK interior should balance furniture, storage, movement and the daily requirements of the household.

Living Room

Plan seating according to the available area and keep enough circulation space around furniture.

Bedrooms

Consider bed placement, wardrobes, storage, lighting and comfortable movement.

Kitchen

Focus on workflow, storage and accessibility while selecting the kitchen layout.

Small Spaces

Making a Small Home Feel More Functional

Smaller homes can benefit from thoughtful furniture proportions, storage and circulation planning.

01

Use Proportional Furniture

Avoid oversized furniture that unnecessarily reduces usable floor space.

02

Improve Storage

Consider storage solutions that use available vertical and underused areas.

03

Keep Movement Clear

Furniture placement should allow comfortable movement between important areas.

04

Use Lighting Carefully

Layered lighting can help create a comfortable and visually balanced environment.

Kitchen Design

Modern Modular Kitchen Design Ideas

A good kitchen layout should make everyday cooking, storage and movement practical.

Workflow

Place frequently used kitchen zones so movement between them remains practical.

Storage

Plan drawers, cabinets and other storage based on the items that will actually be used.

Materials

Select finishes according to appearance, maintenance requirements and intended use.

Bedroom Design

Modern Bedroom Interior Design Ideas

Bedrooms should balance comfort, storage, lighting and personal preferences.

Comfortable Layout

Keep the bed and other furniture positioned to maintain comfortable circulation.

Practical Storage

Wardrobes and other storage should fit the available space without making the room feel crowded.

Lighting

Combine functional and ambient lighting for different activities and moods.

Creating a Balanced Living Room

01

Seating

02

Storage

Consider practical storage without overcrowding the main living area.

03

Lighting

Use lighting to support both everyday activities and the overall atmosphere.

04

Visual Balance

Coordinate furniture, materials and colours to create a cohesive room.

Choosing a Designer

How to Choose an Interior Designer

Choosing an interior designer should involve understanding the project requirements and comparing relevant design approaches.

01

Review Relevant Work

Look at projects or concepts that are relevant to the type of space you want to design.

02

Discuss Requirements

Explain your property, lifestyle, requirements, preferences and project priorities.

03

Understand the Process

Ask how design planning, materials, timelines and project stages will be handled.

04

Compare the Fit

Consider whether the designer's approach matches the needs of your particular project.
