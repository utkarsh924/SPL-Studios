Interior Design Services Questions

What types of interior design projects do you handle?

The service structure covers residential spaces such as homes, 2BHK and 3BHK apartments, villas, kitchens, bedrooms and living rooms, along with offices, restaurants, cafés and other commercial spaces.

Yes. The consultation stage is designed to understand the property, requirements, preferences and goals before planning the design direction.

Yes. The residential service structure includes planning considerations for both 2BHK and 3BHK homes, including room layout, storage, furniture and everyday functionality.

Can you work on commercial interiors?

The commercial service structure covers offices, restaurants, cafés, shops and other business environments where layout and functionality are important.
