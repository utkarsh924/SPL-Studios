07 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start, here's what you may want to know.

What type of interior projects do you handle?

SPL Studios works across residential and commercial interiors, including homes, apartments, kitchens, bedrooms, living rooms, offices, restaurants and other commercial spaces.

Do you work on 2BHK and 3BHK homes?

Yes. Apartment interiors can include space planning, furniture layouts, storage, modular kitchens, bedrooms, living areas and other room-by-room requirements.

Can I discuss only one room?

Yes. You can discuss an individual space such as a living room, bedroom or kitchen as well as a larger home or commercial project.

How can I discuss my interior project?

You can use the consultation form to share your property details, requirements and project goals.
