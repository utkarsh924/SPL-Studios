Questions

Consultation FAQs

What can I discuss during an interior design consultation?

You can discuss your property, space planning, room requirements, kitchen, furniture, lighting, colours, materials, renovation needs and overall design direction.

Do you handle both residential and commercial interiors?

The planned service structure includes residential and commercial interior requirements such as homes, offices, restaurants, cafés and commercial spaces.

What information should I provide?

Sharing the property type, approximate size, rooms or areas involved and your main requirements can make the initial discussion more useful.

Can I discuss only one room?

Yes. A project can focus on a specific space such as a living room, bedroom or kitchen, depending on the requirements.

Is the enquiry form currently connected to a booking system?

The website form is currently prepared for a real enquiry system, but a live backend or form service must be connected before the website is launched for actual enquiries.
